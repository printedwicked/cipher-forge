CipherForge

LOCAL · BROWSER-ONLY

A crypto pen-test bench. Forge a payload, encrypt it with anything from a Caesar shift to AES-GCM, then throw your own ciphertext at the attack toolbox and watch which schemes fold.

Everything runs in this page. Your payload never leaves the browser — you encrypt your own data and attack your own output. This is a learning rig for seeing why weak crypto breaks, not a cracker for other people's files. The toolbox folds toy ciphers in milliseconds and bounces straight off AES; that contrast is the whole point.

Attack the ciphertext

      no key supplied — the toolbox works blind
    

    

      
Runs every attack against whatever is currently in the ciphertext buffer, assuming nothing about which method you used. Weak schemes surrender their key and plaintext; strong ones give the toolbox nothing but noise.

      

        
        
      

      

    

  


  

    Attacks implemented: single-byte XOR brute-force (256-key, English-scored) · repeating-key XOR (Hamming-distance keysize detection → per-column brute, CryptoPals set 1/6) · Caesar brute · frequency analysis · ECB repeated-block detection · Shannon entropy.
